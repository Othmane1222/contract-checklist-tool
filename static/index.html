How it works

  1. Upload your contract

    A PDF or Word file, in Chinese or in two languages.

  2. We check it against a checklist

    Each contract type has its own list of key terms. Every finding quotes the clause it is based on.

  3. Read the results in your language

    English, Spanish or Chinese, with risks marked and a plain-language explanation of each clause.

We read your file to check it, and then it's gone.
