How it works

  1. Upload your contract

    A PDF or Word file, in Chinese or in two languages.

  2. We check it against a checklist

    Each contract type has its own list of key terms. Every finding quotes the clause it is based on.

  3. Read the results in your language

    English, Spanish or Chinese, with risks marked and a plain-language explanation of each clause.

How we handle your contract

Here is exactly what happens to your contract.

  • Your file is deleted from memory after processing. It is not written to disk or to logs on our server.
  • The contract text is sent to our AI provider, DeepSeek, for analysis.
  • We never attach your contract to messages sent to a lawyer.

Need a lawyer?

After your check, you can send a short message to a lawyer from the results page. Your contract is never attached.
